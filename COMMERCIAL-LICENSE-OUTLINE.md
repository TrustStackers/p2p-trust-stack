This outlines the commercial license offered as an alternative to the
[GPL-3.0](./LICENSE) license for `@p2p-trust-stack/core`.

## What the commercial license covers

A paid commercial license grants the licensee the right to use, modify, and
distribute the software **without** the obligations of GPL-3.0 — specifically
relief from:

- Copyleft / derivative-work obligations (§5)
- Source-code disclosure and "offer of source" requirements
- The requirement to license the licensee's own modifications under GPL-3.0

It also typically adds commercial assurances the GPL does not provide.

## Key terms to define (with counsel)

| Term | Considerations |
|---|---|
| Licensee & scope | Named legal entity; subsidiaries/affiliates included or not |
| Grant | Internal use, redistribution, embedding, or hosting as a service |
| Territory & term | Perpetual vs. annual subscription; worldwide or restricted |
| Quantities | Per-node, per-seat, per-organization, or unlimited |
| Warranty | Limited warranty vs. "AS IS" |
| Indemnification | IP infringement indemnity (limited, capped) |
| Support | SLA tiers, response times, LTS/backport policy |
| Updates | Included for term; version lock options |
| Termination | Breach, non-payment, change-of-control |
| Audit | Self-certification vs. audit rights |
| Trademark | Separate trademark license (not included by default) |

## Model recommendations

- **Annual subscription** (recurring revenue) rather than perpetual.
- **Tiered** by scale (e.g., per-node pricing) with an enterprise tier.
- **Self-serve** on a pricing page for the low tiers; sales-assisted for
  enterprise.
